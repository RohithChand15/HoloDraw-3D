HOLODraw 3D — Natural Gesture Manual

Control your studio naturally using laptop webcam hand gestures, with seamless mouse & keyboard fallback.

☝️ Index Finger Raised DRAW

Raise index finger. Freehand paints in 2D or generates continuous 3D spatial volumetric tubes.

🤏 Thumb + Index Pinch SELECT & MOVE

Hover over an object (turns gold) and pinch to grab! Move your hand across X/Y space, or push/pull depth.

👐 Two-Hand Dual Pinch SCALE & ROTATE

Pinch with both hands. Expanding hands scales object (0.25 to 4.5); tilting hands rotates smoothly.

✋ Open Palm PAUSE & HOVER

Open all fingers to safely hover across the workspace without accidental painting or dragging.

✊ Closed Fist CANCEL / DESELECT

Clench fist to cancel active stroke or deselect currently active object.

🗑️ Trash Zone Release DELETE & UNDO

Drag object over bottom-right portal. Turns glowing red. Release pinch to delete. Press Ctrl+Z to undo!
